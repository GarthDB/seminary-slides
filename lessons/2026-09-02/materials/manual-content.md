# Lesson 169: Actions That Invite the Holy Ghost into Scripture Study

**Course:** Old Testament Seminary Student Manual (2026)
**Section:** 62 — Scripture Study Skills (624)
**Source:** https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/old-testament-seminary-student-manual-2026/62-scripture-study-skills/624-actions-that-invite-the-holy-ghost?lang=eng

## Lesson purpose

To practice skills that invite the Holy Ghost into scripture study.

## Introduction

Opens with a "home-field advantage" sports analogy — the environment where you study
affects how well the Holy Ghost can help you understand and apply the scriptures.
Students are invited to prepare spiritually before studying.

## Actions That Invite the Holy Ghost into Scripture Study

Two contrasting scenarios: **Ada**, who struggles to find consistent study time amid a
busy schedule, and **Zuri**, who wakes early to study daily.

**Key principle:** As you make deliberate efforts to approach your scripture study
carefully, Heavenly Father will meet you where you are and send the Holy Ghost to help
you recognize truth (see 2 Nephi 32:3, 5; John 14:26).

Three approaches to inviting the Holy Ghost into scripture study:

### 1. Removing distractions
Read Matthew 14:22–23 — how Jesus Christ managed distractions and stayed focused
(withdrawing to a mountain alone to pray). Discuss why removing distractions matters
and what strategies students could use.

### 2. Prayer
President Dallin H. Oaks:

> "Before I begin to read the scriptures each day, I bow my head, and ask the Lord to
> bless me that I will have His Spirit to enlighten me on the meaning of what I am
> reading and to be with me throughout the day to help me make decisions, and to resist
> temptation."
> — *Welcome to Seminary* (2012)

Reflect on using prayer to show a desire for connection with Heavenly Father.

### 3. Pondering the scriptures
**Guide to the Scriptures:** ponder means "to meditate and think deeply, often upon the
scriptures or other things of God."

Elder D. Todd Christofferson:

> "Care more about the amount of time you spend in the scriptures than about the amount
> you read in that time."
> — "When Thou Art Converted," *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2004, 11

Recommended pattern: read a verse or two, stop to ponder, re-read carefully, pray for
understanding, and write down impressions.

## Guided practice activity

Five minutes practicing these skills with Isaiah 55:1–3, 8–11 (or a passage of choice):

1. Remove distractions
2. Open with prayer
3. Read and ponder verses
4. Re-read carefully
5. Pray for understanding
6. Wait for and record impressions

## Closing discussion

Share with your teacher or class what you learned or felt — and what you would share
with Ada or Zuri.
