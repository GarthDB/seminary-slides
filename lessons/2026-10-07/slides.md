<!--
Presenter Notes:
- About 4 minutes. Divide the passages among pairs or small groups so several are represented.
- This is a direct-scripture adaptation of the manual's handout activity, not a transcription of the handout.
- Students may also use “The Lord's Invitations and Promises to His Covenant People,” linked from the teacher manual.
- Helps if needed: 51:4–8 invites listening and not fearing others' reproach, with enduring salvation; 51:12–16 reminds people of their Maker, His comfort, and His care.
- Isaiah 52:1–3 calls Zion to awake and arise and speaks of redemption. Isaiah 52:11–12 invites leaving uncleanness and promises the Lord will go before and behind His people.
- “Rearward” means rear guard. “Reproach” refers to criticism or disapproval. Avoid interpreting “fear not” as shame for feeling anxiety.
- If a passage is difficult, help students put one phrase into their own words rather than rushing them.
-->
