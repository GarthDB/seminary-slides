# It's hard to recognize truth

# "Call evil good, and good evil"

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed">

Through the prophet Isaiah, the Lord warned of a problem we would face in our day that would make it hard to recognize truth. Read [Isaiah 5:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/5?lang=eng&id=p20#p20), looking for what the Lord taught.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg">

**Discuss:**
- When have you seen or read something that made you question whether it was true?
- What can make it difficult to recognize truth in our day?

</div>

</div>

# Be cautious as you seek truth

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed">

President Dallin H. Oaks of the First Presidency taught:

> "We live in a time of greatly expanded and disseminated information. But not all of this information is true. We need to be cautious as we seek truth and choose sources for that search. We should not consider secular prominence or authority as qualified sources of truth. We should be cautious about relying on information or advice offered by entertainment stars, prominent athletes, or anonymous internet sources. Expertise in one field should not be taken as expertise on truth in other subjects."
>
> — ["Truth and the Plan," *Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2018, 25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2018/10/truth-and-the-plan?lang=eng)

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg">

**Ask yourself:** How confident do you feel in your ability to recognize truth? Who can you trust to give you accurate answers to gospel and Church-related questions?

</div>

</div>

# Discern between truth and error

<!-- Presenter Notes: Transition into the account of Moses and Satan -->

---
layout: default

# Moses discerns between Satan and God

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed">

After Moses spoke with God face to face, Satan came and attempted to deceive Moses into worshipping him (see [Moses 1:12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?lang=eng&id=p12#p12)).

Read [Moses 1:13–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?lang=eng&id=p13-p16#p13), looking for how Moses was able to discern between Satan and God.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg">

**Discuss:**
- What did you find?
- How can we relate Moses's experience to our lives as we choose sources to find truth and answers to our questions?
- What are you grateful to know that you wouldn't know without the divinely appointed sources Heavenly Father has provided in our day? Why?

</div>

</div>

<!-- Presenter Notes: Transition into the small-group activity -->

# Correct the false statement

<div class="space-y-3 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-4 bg-red-50 rounded-lg">

**Choose one false statement (or one of your own):**
1. God doesn't really care how we live as long as we don't hurt others.
2. This life is all there is — we didn't exist before we were born, and we won't exist after we die.
3. We really don't need the Book of Mormon since we already have the Bible.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-green-50 rounded-lg">

**Using a divinely appointed source, find statements that correct it.** Write what you find in your study journal. Consider:
- Doctrinal mastery passages, Guide to the Scriptures, Topical Guide, or scripture index
- Topics and Questions at ChurchofJesusChrist.org
- General conference addresses · Gospel Library · your parents or a Church leader

</div>

</div>
