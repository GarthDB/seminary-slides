# What Has the Lord Revealed?

<div class="p-4 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-lg text-gray-800">
Pick one or two questions. Search the scriptures for answers.
</div>

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-base">

1. What does it mean that the Lord will "create new heavens and a new earth"? ([Isaiah 65:17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/65?lang=eng&id=p17#p17))
2. Will there be death during the Millennium? ([Isaiah 65:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/65?lang=eng&id=p20#p20))
3. What will happen to Satan?
4. What roles will the Savior have?
5. What else has the Lord revealed?

</div>

<!--
Presenter Notes:
- About 4 minutes of silent or paired study. Students choose a question of their own or one from the list.
- Helpful resources: "Millennium" in Guide to the Scriptures and Topics and Questions on ChurchofJesusChrist.org.
- Reading for each question (also on the next slide):
  1. New heavens and new earth: Doctrine and Covenants 88:25–26; Articles of Faith 1:10. Think about what these verses teach about the Savior's power and His feelings for the righteous.
  2. Death in the Millennium: Doctrine and Covenants 101:29–31. Ponder what these verses teach about the Savior's power.
  3. Satan: Doctrine and Covenants 45:55; 101:28; 1 Nephi 22:26; Revelation 20:1–3. Think about what they teach about the Lord.
  4. Roles of the Savior: Doctrine and Covenants 29:11; 45:59; Articles of Faith 1:10.
  5. More revealed: Doctrine and Covenants 45:54, 56–58; 101:26–29; "Millennium" in Topics and Questions.
- Invite students to ponder any questions they have about the Millennium and the blessings of the Savior's return.
-->
