## Meta description

This lesson can help students feel an increased excitement for the Savior’s Second Coming and millennial reign.

## Plain text outline (from manual body)

```text
With the world in turmoil, we all long for moments of peace. The Savior Jesus Christ will return to the earth and reign for a thousand years, ushering in a time of peace. Isaiah prophesied of this period, which is called the Millennium. This lesson can help students feel an increased excitement for the Savior’s Second Coming and millennial reign.
Student preparation:
Invite students to consider what they know about the Savior’s Second Coming and Millennium reign. Invite them to ponder if they look forward to it and why. They might ask a parent or trusted adult how they feel about the Savior’s return.
Possible Learning Activities
The Millennium
To help students understand why the return of the Savior will be such a blessing in our lives, consider beginning by asking the following question:
What are some problems in the world that you wish would change?
You could write some of the following phrases on the board to help students think of answers:
Reasons people weep or hurt
Examples of violence, unfairness, or destruction
The influence of Satan
Take a moment to ponder any problems in your life or in the lives of those you love that you wish would change. As you study today, seek the guidance of the Holy Ghost as you look for truths that may help us and the world.
The final chapters of Isaiah
Show a picture of the Savior’s Second Coming and explain that the scriptures teach that in the future, the Savior will return to the earth and reign for a thousand years. This period is called the Millennium.
```
