# With the end in mind

<div class="p-6 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-lg leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto text-gray-900">

Sometimes it can be easy to focus on what happens in this life while forgetting to view our lives from an eternal perspective. In Ecclesiastes, the Preacher reminds us that the things of this world are temporary. He counsels us to prepare for when God will judge all of His children.

</div>

# What President Nelson taught

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-6 bg-gradient-to-r from-amber-50 to-yellow-50 rounded-xl text-base leading-relaxed border border-amber-200 text-gray-900">

President **Russell M. Nelson** taught:

*"Every righteous choice that you make here will pay huge dividends now. But righteous choices in mortality will pay unimaginable dividends eternally. … The adversary, of course, does not want you even to think about tomorrow, let alone eternal life."*
— "Choices for Eternity" (worldwide devotional for young adults, May 15, 2022), Gospel Library

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** How might failing to view our choices with an eternal perspective affect us?

</div>

</div>

# Write it down

<div class="p-6 bg-purple-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto space-y-3 text-gray-900">

Write down a decision you are currently facing that could "pay unimaginable dividends eternally." A few ideas, if you need them:

- Whether to continue spending time with a certain group of friends
- Whether to repent of a sin that has been on your mind

**Ponder:** Why might Heavenly Father want you to think about the future as you make this decision? What might happen if you don't?

# The Preacher

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

In the book of Ecclesiastes, the author referred to himself as "the Preacher" ([Ecclesiastes 1:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/1?lang=eng&id=p1#p1)). The Preacher contrasted the emptiness of focusing our lives on temporal things with the importance of preparing to meet God.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Key terms:**
- "Vanity" means empty or fleeting
- "Under heaven" or "under the sun" means in this mortal life
- "Vexation of spirit" means frustrating

</div>

</div>

<!--
Presenter Notes:
- Consider small groups, each reading one of the two passages on the next slide, substituting these definitions as they read.
-->

---
layout: default
---

# Read and look for...

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

Read the following passages, looking for what the Preacher discovered after pursuing certain things that some people think will make them happy and bring fulfillment:

- **Wisdom of the world** — [Ecclesiastes 1:13–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/1?lang=eng&id=p13-p18#p13)
- **Temporal pleasures and material possessions** — [Ecclesiastes 2:1, 4–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/2?lang=eng&id=p1,p4-p11#p1)

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** Worldly wisdom and material possessions aren't necessarily bad — but when they become our focus, especially over pleasing God, they become spiritually dangerous. In what ways might pursuing these things eventually leave us feeling empty or frustrated?

</div>

</div>

# Preparing to be judged

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

After seeking temporal knowledge and possessions, the Preacher ends his book with the lessons he learned. Read [Ecclesiastes 12:7, 13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/eccl/12?lang=eng&id=p7,p13-p14#p7), looking for what he taught that can help us keep an eternal perspective.

This perspective is so important that God has inspired many of His servants to teach it. Cross-reference with [Alma 34:32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/34?lang=eng&id=p32#p32) and [Mormon 3:20, 22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/3?lang=eng&id=p20,p22#p20) — how did Amulek and Mormon teach a similar message?

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** How would you summarize what these verses teach in a statement of truth?
*(Truth to help students identify: Our choices in this life will determine how prepared we are to stand before God at the Final Judgment.)*

</div>

</div>

# Our Judge, through Christ

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** What attributes of God uniquely qualify Him to be our Judge?

</div>

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

Read [3 Nephi 27:16, 19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/27?lang=eng&id=p16,p19#p16), looking for how we can prepare to meet Jesus Christ at the Final Judgment.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** Why is it important to understand that we cannot prepare for the Final Judgment without Jesus Christ?

</div>

# Beginning with the end in mind

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-6 bg-gradient-to-r from-amber-50 to-yellow-50 rounded-xl text-base leading-relaxed border border-amber-200 text-gray-900">

President **Russell M. Nelson** testified:

*"In my first message as President of the Church, I encouraged you to begin with the end in mind. This means making the celestial kingdom your eternal goal and then carefully considering where each of your decisions while here on earth will place you in the next world."*
— "Think Celestial!," *Liahona*, Nov. 2023, 118

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** In what ways do Jesus Christ's teachings and example show that He made decisions "with the end in mind"?

# Small group practice

<div class="p-6 bg-purple-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto space-y-3 text-gray-900">
