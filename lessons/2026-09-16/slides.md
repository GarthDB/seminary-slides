# Be cautious as you seek truth

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

President Dallin H. Oaks taught: "We need to be cautious as we seek truth and choose sources" (Ensign or Liahona, Nov. 2018, 25).

**Five questions for evaluating a source's reliability:**

1. What are the author's qualifications, intentions, and biases?
2. How directly connected is the author to the events described?
3. Does the author intentionally ignore available evidence?
4. Are teachings presented with proper historical context?
5. Are events supported by additional reliable sources?

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Applied scenario:** Evaluate a fictional "Journey West" journal for reliability using the five questions above.

</div>

</div>
