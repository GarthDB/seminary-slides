# The Shepherd in Morocco

<div class="p-4 bg-yellow-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-800">

On a trip to Morocco, Elder John R. Lasater learned about the Savior's love when a sheep was hit by a royal car, entitling the shepherd to a large payment. The shepherd would not take the money. Elder Lasater asked why:

“Because of the love he has for each of his sheep.” … The old shepherd reached down, lifted the injured lamb in his arms, and placed it in a large pouch on the front of his robe. He kept stroking its head, repeating the same word over and over again. … “He is calling it by name. All of his sheep have a name, for he is their shepherd, and the good shepherds know each one of their sheep by name.”

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-sm">
Elder John R. Lasater, “Shepherds of Israel,” Ensign, May 1988, 74
</div>

# How Can We Care Like He Does?

<div class="p-4 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-lg text-gray-800">
In small groups, use Ezekiel 34:11–16 to list practical ways youth can care for others as the Savior does.
</div>

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-lg">

- “I will … seek them out” → visit someone you haven't seen in a while
- “I will feed my flock” → be generous with your time or resources
- “I will … bring again that which was driven away” → invite someone to join you
- “I will … bind up that which was broken” → notice others' physical or emotional needs

</div>

# Make a Plan

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-lg text-gray-800">
Think of someone who may be like the sheep in Ezekiel 34: lost, scattered, broken, or weak. They might be in your class, your family, or your school. Ask the Holy Ghost who needs your help.
</div>

<div class="mt-4 space-y-2 text-lg">

- **How** will you follow the Savior's example for them?
