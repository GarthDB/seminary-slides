# Reading Isaiah 11–12

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

Read [Isaiah 11:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/11?lang=eng&id=p1#p1) (the stem/rod/root imagery), [11:6–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/11?lang=eng&id=p6-p9#p6) (the Savior and the Millennium), and [11:10–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/11?lang=eng&id=p10-p12#p10) (the root of Jesse and the gathering work).

**Reading tip:** "Set his hand again the second time" ([11:11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/11?lang=eng&id=p11#p11)) refers to the latter-day gathering of Israel. An ensign is a flag or banner used to gather armies — the Restoration of the Church is the prophesied ensign.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Cross-reference:** [Doctrine and Covenants 113:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/113?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1) — Joseph Smith's questions and answers explain the symbols:

- **Stem** = Jesus Christ ([D&C 113:1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/113?lang=eng&id=p1-p2#p1))
- **Rod** = Joseph Smith ([D&C 113:3–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/113?lang=eng&id=p3-p4#p3))
- **Root of Jesse** = Joseph Smith ([D&C 113:5–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/113?lang=eng&id=p5-p6#p5))

Also see [Doctrine and Covenants 137:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/137?lang=eng&id=p6#p6) and [Doctrine and Covenants 45:9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/45?lang=eng&id=p9#p9).

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Truth:** The Lord's restored Church is an ensign to gather people to the gospel of Jesus Christ.

</div>

</div>

# Design a flag for the gathering

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

Design a flag (an "ensign") that represents the restored gospel. Include:

- A representation of Jesus Christ
- Distinctives of the restored Church — the Book of Mormon, modern prophets, priesthood, temples
- How the Lord blesses His children through these elements

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Share:** Present your flag and explain what you included and why.

</div>

</div>

# Building friendships that gather

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

President Henry B. Eyring shared an account of an elders quorum president who helped gather others by:

- Building genuine friendships with less-active members
- Listening without judgment during hard times
- Sharing spiritual solutions when people were receptive

*(Optional video: Elder Gary E. Stevenson's story on gospel sharing.)*

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** Who is someone you could reach out to this week, and what is one small, genuine way you could start?

</div>

# Discussion questions

<div class="p-8 bg-gradient-to-r from-blue-50 to-purple-50 rounded-xl text-left text-lg space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-gray-900">

1. **What do the stem, rod, and root of Jesse represent, and why does that matter to you?**

2. **How would you summarize what Isaiah 11:10–12 teaches as a statement of truth?**

3. **What is one element of the restored Church you'd put on your "ensign," and why?**

4. **Who is someone you could build a genuine friendship with this week?**

5. **What have you felt impressed to do to help gather others to the gospel?**

</div>
