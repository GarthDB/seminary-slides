# Which label is really you?

<div class="space-y-6 max-w-3xl mx-auto text-lg">

<div class="p-8 bg-gradient-to-r from-blue-50 to-cyan-50 rounded-xl text-left">

Imagine a young woman in your class who seems to do a little of everything — she plays soccer, gets good grades, has followers on social media, sings in the choir, watches her siblings, and is a patient, understanding friend.

But what you don't know is she sometimes feels **confused about who she really is**. Is she an athlete? A student? An influencer? It can be hard to figure out.

</div>

<div class="text-sm text-gray-600 italic">
What labels do you place on yourself? It may be worth recording them somewhere so you can look at them later.
</div>

</div>

<!--
Presenter Notes:
- Let the scenario land before moving on — most students will recognize themselves in her.
- Transition: "As we study today, look for how the Lord wants you to see yourself. Let the Holy Ghost help you see yourself as Heavenly Father does."
-->

# The most important label

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-6 bg-gradient-to-r from-amber-50 to-yellow-50 rounded-xl text-base leading-relaxed border border-amber-200">

President **Russell M. Nelson** wrote:

*"Labels can be fun and indicate your support for any number of positive things. But if any label replaces your most important identifiers, the results can be spiritually suffocating. I believe that if the Lord were speaking to you directly, the first thing He would make sure you understand is your true identity. My dear friends, you are literally spirit children of God."*
— Instagram, July 20, 2022 (instagram.com/russellmnelson)

</div>

</div>

<!--

# Three labels that never let you down

<div class="max-w-2xl mx-auto text-left space-y-4">

<div class="p-6 bg-indigo-50 rounded-xl text-lg leading-relaxed">

President Nelson: no identifier should displace, replace, or take priority over these three enduring designations —

1. **Child of God**
2. **Child of the covenant**
3. **Disciple of Jesus Christ**

*"Any identifier that is not compatible with those three basic designations will ultimately let you down. Make no mistake about it: your potential is divine."*

</div>

</div>

<!--

- Ask: which of your other labels are good and true, but shouldn't outrank these three?

# Seeing others as children of God

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-purple-50 rounded-lg leading-relaxed">

Seeing *yourself* as a child of God can change how you view and treat *yourself and others*. President Nelson pleaded:

*"Each of us has a divine potential because each is a child of God. Each is equal in His eyes. ... God does not love one race more than another. ... I plead with you to promote respect for all of God's children."*
— "Let God Prevail," *Liahona*, Nov. 2020, 94

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg">

**Discuss:** How would seeing others as children of God help you follow this counsel?

</div>

</div>

<!--

# "I am awesome! I am a child of God!"

<div class="space-y-6 max-w-3xl mx-auto text-lg">

<div class="p-8 bg-gradient-to-r from-rose-50 to-amber-50 rounded-xl text-left">

Elder **Gary E. Stevenson** of the Quorum of the Twelve invited youth:

*"Many of you start your day by standing in front of a mirror. Tomorrow, this week, this year, always, pause as you look at yourself in the mirror. Think to yourself, or say aloud if you like, 'Wow, look at me! I am awesome! I am a child of God! He knows me! He loves me!'"*
— "Promptings of the Spirit," *Liahona*, Nov. 2023, 45

</div>

<div class="text-sm text-gray-600 italic">
It may feel awkward at first — but there is real power in saying these truths aloud. Consider trying it right now.
</div>

</div>

<!--

# Discussion

<div class="p-6 bg-gradient-to-r from-blue-50 to-purple-50 rounded-xl text-left text-base space-y-5 max-w-4xl mx-auto">

1. **President Nelson said any label incompatible with "Child of God, Child of the covenant, Disciple of Jesus Christ" will "ultimately let you down."** What's a label — even a good one — that could crowd out your sense of who you really are?

2. **Moses's knowledge that he was a child of God shaped how he responded to Satan.** How does remembering your own divine identity change how you respond to hard moments?

3. **"How would seeing others as children of God help you follow the Lord's counsel?"** — What's one relationship this week where that view could make a difference?

</div>

<!--
