# A car without reverse

<div class="space-y-6 max-w-3xl mx-auto text-lg">

<div class="p-8 bg-gradient-to-r from-blue-50 to-cyan-50 rounded-xl text-left">

A few years back, money was tight for one man. The car he used to get to work was barely holding on — most notably, the transmission refused to shift into reverse. With no way to back up, he began planning every trip to make sure he always had a way to keep moving forward. To his surprise, the planning worked pretty well.

Eventually he got the car repaired. But he'd already learned the lesson: **life is like a car without reverse.** If you want to keep moving forward, you have to plan for the future. *(Chad O., 37, Washington State, US)*

# Four steps to set and reach goals

<div class="space-y-3 text-base max-w-4xl mx-auto">

1. **Prayerfully set goals and make plans.** Choose goals that are realistic but that stretch you and require faith — not too high, not too low.
2. **Record and schedule.** Write your goals and plans into a detailed schedule.
3. **Act on your plans.** Work diligently, and exercise faith in the Lord to help you.
4. **Review and follow up.** Evaluate your progress regularly. Decide what to improve, and adjust as needed.

</div>

# Jayden tries the process

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto space-y-2">

- Jayden asks Heavenly Father for help setting a goal. He decides to study the Book of Mormon daily for 10 minutes, and to wake up earlier so he can study before school.
- After one week, he notices he keeps sleeping in. He prays for help.
- After two weeks, he feels like he's failed. He asks Heavenly Father to help him feel Their love for him — and feels encouraged to try again, trusting Their help, this time going to bed earlier so waking up is easier.

</div>

# Practice goal setting

<div class="p-6 bg-purple-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto">
