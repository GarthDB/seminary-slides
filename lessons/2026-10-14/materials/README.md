## Teaching this deck

- The 18-slide deck follows a 40-minute flow with 10 minutes of student-led QT Time.
- Core readings: Mark 2:27 and the doctrinal mastery passage Isaiah 58:13–14.
- Students find invitations and promises, practice the doctrinal mastery reference and key phrase, and plan one realistic way to honor the Lord on the Sabbath.
- President Russell M. Nelson's question is quoted and linked; the surrounding teaching is summarized.
- Presenter notes emphasize personal guidance, varied family circumstances, and avoiding lists of permitted activities or judgments of others.
- No printed handout or video is required. `manual-content.md` preserves the fetched source outline.
- QT Time is led by Joy: "What is one Book of Mormon scripture (of many) that impacts your life?"
