# A collection of poetic wisdom

<div class="p-5 bg-blue-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto text-gray-900">

The book of Proverbs is a collection of poetic wisdom written to the Israelites. Some passages are attributed to King Solomon (see [Proverbs 1:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/1?lang=eng&id=p1#p1); [10:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/10?lang=eng&id=p1#p1); [25:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/25?lang=eng&id=p1#p1); see also [1 Kings 4:32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/4?lang=eng&id=p32#p32)), while other authors are unknown.

Proverbs — like Job and Ecclesiastes — uses elements of Hebrew poetry that can be challenging to read. Many proverbs are deeply spiritual, but some are simply worldly advice. As you study, pay attention to how the Holy Ghost testifies of truth as you identify direction the Lord would like you to apply to your life.

</div>

# The purposes of the proverbs

<div class="space-y-4 max-w-4xl mx-auto text-sm">

<div class="p-5 bg-green-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed text-gray-900">

Read [Proverbs 1:1–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p7#p1), looking for some of the purposes of the proverbs.

**Note:** "the fear of the Lord" ([verse 7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/1?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)) refers to feeling and showing reverence and respect toward Him.

</div>

<div class="p-4 bg-indigo-50 rounded-lg text-gray-900">

**Discuss:** Why do you think reverencing or respecting the Lord might be "the beginning of knowledge"?

# Create a visual

<div class="p-6 bg-purple-50 rounded-lg text-base leading-relaxed max-w-4xl mx-auto space-y-3 text-gray-900">

Imagine you were asked to create a visual that uses one of the Proverbs passages to help teenagers become more like the Savior. Select a Proverb and create a poster — like one you might find in *For the Strength of Youth* magazine. Your visual could contain:

- A simple illustration — something creative that could grab a teenager's attention
- The Proverbs reference and the truth it teaches
- An explanation of how the verse could help a teenager follow the Savior's example or become more like Him
